Retail Intel — Terms of Use

Version 1.0 · 10 September 2026

What this service is

Retail Intel maps every licensed dispensary in New York and ranks each one by what it actually sells. That market layer — store rank, category mix, price tier, momentum — is the baseline, and it is the same for every company using the service.

On top of the baseline, your company can upload its own sales data. That turns the market view into your view: the doors you already sell stop reading as prospects and start carrying your revenue, your order cadence and how long it has been since they last bought.

Your sales data belongs to you

What you upload is private to your company. It is visible only to people you have added to your company, it is never shown to another company, and it is never presented in a form that identifies you as its source.

You keep ownership of it. You can replace it at any time by uploading again, and you can ask us to delete it, which we will do within 30 days.

Give to get

This is the part worth reading closely, because it is the deal.

The baseline is bought from a market-data vendor and it is incomplete by nature: it estimates sell-through, it lags, and it covers only the categories our subscription reaches. Every company that uploads its own sales makes that baseline measurably better — real invoiced figures are the only way to check an estimate.

So: when you upload sales data, you grant us the right to use it internally, in aggregated and anonymised form, to improve the market model that every customer sees.

In practice that means calibrating our sell-through estimates against real invoiced volume, correcting store and product matching, and measuring how far the vendor's numbers drift from what actually shipped.

What we will never do with it

Never shown. Your figures are never displayed to another company — not your revenue, not your accounts, not your order history, not your product mix, at any level of detail that could be traced to you.

Never attributed. Nothing derived from your data carries your name, your brand, or anything that identifies you as its source. Where derived figures reach the shared baseline, they arrive aggregated across companies, not as your row.

Never sold. We do not sell, licence or otherwise pass your data to third parties, and we do not use it to build a product for your competitors to read.

Never a back door. A company cannot query, export, or infer another company's uploads through this service.

If a derived figure could only have come from one company — a category where a single customer is the whole market, say — we leave it out rather than publish it. Anonymisation that only works when a lot of people are in the pool is not anonymisation.

Who can see what, inside your company

  • Admins can upload and replace sales data, add and remove people, set the brands your company sells, and see how the team is using the service.
  • Members see your company's view of the map and their own saved work. They cannot upload or change data.

Anyone you add to your company can see your uploaded figures. Add carefully.

Accounts and access

Access is by email and an access code we issue. Codes are personal — sharing one shares your company's data with whoever you gave it to. Tell us and we will reissue.

We are candid about the strength of this: an emailed access code over HTTPS scopes your company's data to your company and puts a real name on activity. It is not a security boundary of the kind that protects regulated or highly sensitive records, and this service is not the right place for such records.

The baseline is an estimate

Market figures are modelled sell-through from a third-party vendor, not audited sales. They lag, they carry error, and the categories covered can change when the underlying subscription changes — when that happens the service tells you which figures are current and which are carried over from an earlier pull. Route times and distances are estimates too. Use judgement before acting on any single number.

Acceptable use

  • Do not upload data you do not have the right to share.
  • Do not attempt to identify another company's data, or to access a company you are not a member of.
  • Do not resell or redistribute the market baseline; it is licensed to us, not to you.

Changes

If we change these terms in a way that affects how your uploaded data is used, we will tell every admin by email before the change takes effect, and you may delete your data and close your company if you disagree.
